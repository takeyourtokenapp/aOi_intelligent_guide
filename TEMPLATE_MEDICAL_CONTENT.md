## Example: Medulloblastoma

```markdown
# Understanding Medulloblastoma

Medulloblastoma (MB) is the most common malignant brain tumor in children. It starts in the cerebellum, the part of the brain that controls balance and coordination.

## Key Facts

- Affects approximately 500 children per year in the US
- Most common in children aged 5-9 years
- Four molecular subgroups: WNT, SHH, Group 3, Group 4
- Requires combination treatment (surgery, radiation, chemotherapy)

## Why This Matters

Each molecular subgroup behaves differently and needs personalized treatment. Group 3 tumors are especially aggressive and spread in about 50% of cases, making them harder to treat effectively.

## Current Research

Scientists are working on:
- Less toxic treatments that don't harm developing brains
- Targeted therapies for specific tumor subgroups
- AI-powered diagnostics to detect tumor cells during surgery
- New drug combinations that work better with fewer side effects

## How Technology Helps

**AI and Machine Learning**: Help doctors identify tumor subgroups faster and more accurately, leading to better-matched treatments.

**Blockchain**: Enables transparent funding for research, so donations go directly to scientists working on new treatments.

**Quantum Computing**: May help model complex protein interactions to design better drugs.

Always consult with qualified medical professionals for
diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice.

**Source**: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12345678/
**Last Reviewed**: 2025-12-28
**Keywords**: medulloblastoma, pediatric brain tumor, cerebellum, CNS, molecular subgroups
```

## Forbidden Content

**NEVER Include**:
- Specific patient cases or outcomes
- Survival rate predictions for individuals
- Treatment recommendations
- Alternative/unproven treatments
- Graphic descriptions of symptoms or procedures
- Post-mortem details
- Medical conspiracy theories
