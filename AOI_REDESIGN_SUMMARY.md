# aOi Organic Redesign - Quick Summary

**Date**: December 27, 2025
**Status**: ✅ Complete & Production Ready
**Philosophy**: soft + tech + academic

---

## What Changed

### ❌ Removed (Old Design)
- Neon colors (cyan #00F0FF, magenta #FF00FF)
- Gold metallic (#D2A44C)
- Aggressive animations
- Tech-heavy aesthetic
- aOi as "button"

### ✅ Added (New Design)
- Organic color palette (lavender, soft blue, sage, mint, rose)
- Breathing, growth animations
- aOi as living presence
- Nature metaphors (🌱 🌊 🧠)
- Cultural symbols (葵)
- First-person voice
